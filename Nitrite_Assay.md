# Nitrite Assay 
Parsons, T. (2013). *A manual of chemical & biological methods for seawater analysis*. Elsevier

### Materials 
* put how many
* 9 50ml Falcon tubes
* Cuvettes
* Spectrometer (@ 525 nm)
* Pipettes & Tips - 1mL
* Kim Wipes

### Chemicals 
* 2.5g of Sulfilanilamide
* 0.25g of NED
* 1 mM of NO2 Standard 

### Reagent Preparation 
1. Sulfilanilamide
   1. 2.5g in 10% HCl (250 mL = 25 mL HCl + 225 mL Milli-Q)
   2. Store in brown bottle at room temperature 
2. NED = N-1-Naphylethylenediamine Dihydrochloride
   1. 0.25 g in 250 mL MQ
   2. Store in brown bottle at room temperature
3. NO2 1000 nM standard
   1. 4.6 µL of 1 mM NO2 working stock (that is stored in the fridge) into 100 mL Milli-Q water
   2. Mix well 


### Material Preparation 
1. NO2 Working Stock
   1. Weigh ~ 0.25g of NaNO2 into a 50 mL Falcon tube
   2. Add up to 50 mL water, mix well
   3. Calculate exact concentration
      - ____g/(69g/mol*50mL)*1,000,000 = mM
   4. Make Secondary (working) stock from primary stock
      - C1 x V1 = C2 x V2
      - (Primary stock concentration) x ______ = 1000 µM x 50 mL
      - ____ µL = (1000*50/(primary stock concentration)
      - So add ____ µL to 49.31 water
2. Label 9 x 50mL Falcon Tubes as follows:

| 1000 nM  | 125 nM | 15.625 nM |
| -- | -- | -- |
| 500 nM  | 62.5 nM  | 7.8125 nM  |
| 250 nM  | 31.25 nM  |  0 nM  |


### Primary Procedure 
1. Label 9 x 50mL Falcon Tubes
2. Rinse all standard Falcon tubes 3x each with DI water before use
3. Add 25mL clean water to each tube, except the 1000 nM tube
4. Add 50 µL of the prepared 1000 µM NO2 standard into the labeled 1000 nM Falcon tube
5. Take 25 mL from the 1000 nM tube and add to the "500 nM" tube. Mix well.
6. Continuem using serial dilutions by adding 25 mL of each previous tube to the 25 mL of water in the next
7. When there is 50 mL in the "7.8125 nM" tube, discard 25 mL. Now all tubes should contain 25 mL

#### <ins>Adding colorimetric reagents to standard curve and samples </ins>
1. To each 25 mL tube of samples/standard, add 500 µL of sufilanilamide first
   - Incubate 2-10 mins in the dark
2. Then add 500 µL of NED
   - Incubate 30 mins - 2 hours before quantifying
  
#### <ins> Running Samples </ins>
1. Make sure the spectrophoto meter is empty 
2. Turn the spectrophotometer on, press the "connect" button, wait for calibration to finish (~ 2 mins) and set to 525 nm for ~ 30 mins before starting analysis
3. Rinse cuvette and add the blank (0 nM NO2 standard) to the cuvette. Use a kim wipe to remove any liquid/fingerprints from the outside
4. Place the cell in the holder
5. Make sure that the cell is always placed in the same direction
   - Keep nozzle to the right and perpendicular to the bench
   - gently push to the left
   - gently push cell forward 
7. When satisfied with zero absorbance, press the "blank"
8. Remove the cuvette
9. Pour the liquid out of the cuvette, rinse, and fill with next standard/sample.
10. Measure and record the absorbance
11. Repeat steps 8-10 for all standards and samples.
12. Once done, rinse and store the cuvette, and turn off the spectrophotometer
